### Week 6 Achievements:

- **Automated Monitoring & Alerting System:** The highlight of this week is that the system can now "self-report" when issues occur — no manual log watching required. Categorizing alarms into Warning/Critical levels avoids "alarm fatigue": minor/temporary errors go to the info channel, while user-impacting issues (DLQ backlog, Bedrock throttling) trigger immediate Slack alerts (`#rag-alerts`).

- **Automated RAG Quality Evaluation (RAGAS):** The RAGAS evaluation yielded unexpected value: after the very first test run, it caught 2 answers with low context faithfulness — something hard to spot manually because the generated answers still read smoothly and logically. This highlights the true value of quantitative evaluation over subjective feel.

- **Completion of 4 Architecture Flows:** By the end of the week, Flow 3 (Monitoring/Alert) and Flow 4 (RAG Evaluation) were fully automated, completing all 4 flows of the overall architecture.
